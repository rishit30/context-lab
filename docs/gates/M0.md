## 5. Interpretation and limitations

Do not report the cost differences as ContextLab savings. No ContextLab intervention occurred. Trajectory lengths differ, and the first arm on each task wrote the initial cached prefix while later arms reused it. Rotating order distributes first position, but one repeat does not establish matched cache conditions or statistical reliability.

These are small diagnostic coding tasks. ContextLab's maximum counted input per task was 4,713, 4,955 and 5,486 tokens. They are not representative long-context workloads. The original genuine Headroom comparator is still unavailable; the approved pilot explicitly used LangChain threshold editing. Hidden checks are independent of editable public tests but are not claimed resistant to malicious Python.

The threshold-editing comparator was also inactive: all requests stayed below its 12,000-token trigger, and none of its 237 inspected tool-result appearances contained a cleared placeholder. Neither intervention's effectiveness was tested by this pilot.

The offline report in `runs/demo-verified/` remains a **simulation**. Its three rebuilds and recall probes demonstrate mechanics only and must not be mixed into live results.
